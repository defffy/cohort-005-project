# Team Refunds are per Seat

A team Purchase can be refunded one Unclaimed Seat at a time instead of all or nothing, because team buyers often only learn how many Seats they need after buying. There is no volume discount, so every Seat costs the same: the amount paid divided by the number of Seats. The Refund Amount is that per-Seat price times the Unclaimed Seats covered. Claimed Seats are never refunded. The buyer may request more than one Refund on the same Purchase while the Refund Window is open, but only one at a time may be Requested. Seats covered by a Requested Refund cannot be Claimed until an Admin decides.

## Consequences

**If bulk discounts are ever added, this rule must change.** A Refund must not take the Purchase below the Seat count that earned the discount. For example, a buyer who bought 12 Seats at a discount that starts at 8 can refund at most 4 Seats, even if they only need 6. Otherwise buyers could take the discount and then refund back down to fewer Seats than it requires.
